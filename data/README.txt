Fushe-Kuqe aquifer dataset
==========================

File: Fushe_Kuqe_All_Data.xlsx

Ownership, source and permission
--------------------------------
These data are the property of the Albanian Geological Survey and of
Arjan Beqiraj, and are used here WITH THEIR PERMISSION.

- Aquifer-boundary polygon and 43 wells only 32 inside (Gauss-Kruger coordinates, hydraulic
  conductivity K, piezometric heads): Albanian Geological Survey (2024).
- Water-chemistry sheets (5_*, 6_*, 7_*): compiled from
  Cenameri, M. & Beqiraj, A. (2016), Bull. Geol. Soc. Greece 50(2), 665-670,
  doi:10.12681/bgsg.11772.

Please cite the above when using this data.

Coordinate system: Gauss-Kruger (Krasovsky-1940, zone 4).

Sheets
------
1_Summary                 overview
2_All_Wells               ID, X_GaussKruger, Y_GaussKruger, K_m_per_day  (43 wells)
3_Boundary_Coords         X, Y  (boundary polygon, 3411 vertices)
4_Inside_With_K           interior nodes with K
5_Cenameri2016_Chemistry  hydrochemistry (Cenameri & Beqiraj 2016)
6_Cenameri2016_Cl_Trend   chloride trend
7_Cenameri2016_SWI_Facts  SWI summary facts
